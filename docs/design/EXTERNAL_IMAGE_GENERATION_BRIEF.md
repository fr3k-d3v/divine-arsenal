## 2. Art direction — apply to every image

Create a distinctive **dark cyberpunk Divine Arsenal** visual language. This is an atmospheric scholarly instrument, not a generic fantasy game.

### Palette
- Dominant: near-black, midnight navy, deep indigo, electric blue, ultraviolet and saturated violet.
- Secondary light: controlled cyan / ice-blue energy, subtle magenta-violet edge light.
- Metallic accents: restrained antique gold, dark brass or tarnished silver.
- Keep shadows genuinely dark. Avoid bright parchment, beige backgrounds, warm sepia, pastel palettes and broad gold washes.

### Materials and atmosphere
- Obsidian, smoked glass, blackened metal, dark vellum, weathered leather, ink, stone, fine etched metal and subtle holographic instrumentation.
- Cinematic low-key lighting, volumetric haze, fine particles, controlled bloom, deep contrast and carefully placed luminous details.
- Blend historical / sacred / manuscript subject matter with a restrained futuristic archive aesthetic: spectral data-light, precision geometry and subtle instrument-like detailing.
- Make the work feel mysterious, intelligent, powerful, reverent and expensive.
- Use detailed painterly realism or high-end cinematic concept-art rendering; avoid cartoon, anime, plastic 3D, generic AI fantasy gloss and stock-photo appearance.

### Composition and interface compatibility
- Each image must work as a standalone editorial image inside a dark web application.
- One clear primary subject; supporting details must not compete with it.
- Maintain visual hierarchy and uncluttered negative space.
- Do not bake labels, titles, scripture, citations, buttons or UI into the artwork.
- Avoid placing important details at the extreme edges where responsive crops may remove them.
- Keep a consistent lighting language and palette across the entire batch while varying scene composition and subject matter.

### Accuracy and integrity
- These are **original AI illustrations**, never historical facsimiles or documentary evidence.
- Do not depict an invented image as an authentic manuscript, historical portrait, seal, talisman or artefact.
- No exact sacred names, Hebrew constructions, Enochian characters, angelic seals, planetary glyphs, zodiac glyphs, kamea, pentacles, sigils or magical diagrams. Those must be rendered separately from verified source data.
- Books may have abstract, non-legible page marks only. Prefer blank pages or shadowed page surfaces.
- Do not fabricate readable text. Do not use gibberish as decorative lettering.
- Do not claim a specific historical person is accurately portrayed. Use symbolic or environmental depictions instead.

### Negative prompt (apply globally)
`collage, composite, contact sheet, mood board, grid, tiled images, split screen, multi-panel, diptych, triptych, sprite sheet, several assets in one frame, text, typography, lettering, readable writing, fake scripture, fake Hebrew, fake Enochian, invented sigils, invented seals, pentacles, kamea, zodiac glyphs, planetary glyphs, logo, watermark, signature, interface, buttons, bright parchment, beige background, sepia wash, pastel colours, cartoon, anime, low detail, blurry, oversaturated bloom, generic fantasy game art, plastic 3D render`

## 4. Delivery and quality-control checklist

For every generated asset:

1. Confirm it is one standalone image and matches exactly one asset ID.
2. Save using the exact filename in the queue.
3. Preserve the original full-resolution master. Do not overwrite it with a compressed preview.
4. Confirm the required aspect ratio and crop safety.
5. Confirm no text, glyphs, fabricated writing, logos or watermarks have appeared.
6. Check that the image reads clearly at the intended card size and remains legible against a dark UI.
7. Confirm the dark-blue / violet cyberpunk art direction is consistent with the batch.
8. For DA-048 and DA-049, verify seamless tiling on all four edges.
9. For DA-050, verify true alpha transparency and inspect the edge quality against both black and light backgrounds.
10. Produce a manifest with asset ID, filename, dimensions, format, file size, SHA-256 and generation status.
11. Keep rejected generations and their reason in a separate QA log; never silently substitute a different asset.
12. Do not claim an asset is integrated into Divine Arsenal until it has been copied into the repository, referenced by the application and visually tested.
