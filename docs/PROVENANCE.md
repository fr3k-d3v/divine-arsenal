## Scientific versus traditional status

Physical positions / solar event times are astronomical calculations.
Arbatel, lunar-mansion, planetary-hour correspondences, kamea meanings and astrology are
historical/symbolic interpretive frameworks. They do not establish causal forces,
personality traits, or guaranteed safety, success or future outcomes.
Source variants must be edition-tagged and reviewed before being described as authentic.
