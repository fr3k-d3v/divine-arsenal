## Verification vocabulary

CLAIM = source project or product statement.
OBSERVATION = direct tool output or inspection.
EVIDENCE = raw logs/measurements/tests retained with provenance.
VERIFIED FACT = a claim checked against the specified acceptance criteria.
HYPOTHESIS = explanation to test.
SIMULATION = mocked or synthetic demonstration.
UNKNOWN/BLOCKED = not yet substantiated.

No automation permission is ever inferred from an astrological symbol, transit or birth chart.
